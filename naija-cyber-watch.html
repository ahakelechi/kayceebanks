POLICY

The rules now in force

Nigeria's core legal and regulatory instruments
NDPA 2023
Data-protection duties for organisations; fines up to 2% of annual gross revenue or ₦10M, whichever is higher.
NDPC, 2025
CBN cybersecurity framework & levy
Mandates bank CISOs; funds the National Cybersecurity Fund via a 0.005% levy on e-transactions — cut from 0.5% after public backlash.
CBN, 2026
Cybercrimes (Amendment) Act 2024
72-hour CERT reporting, 2-year data retention, expanded surveillance powers, new cyberstalking/disinformation offences.
Federal Republic of Nigeria, 2024
Budapest Convention
Nigeria has been a state party since November 2022, formalising cross-border cyber-cooperation.
Council of Europe, 2022
Also worth knowing: Nigeria's National AI Strategy (NAIS) published Sept 2025 — a 5-year plan with a new National AI Trust for oversight.

Key takeaways

RED

Attacks are fewer but costlier, and increasingly AI-assisted — deepfakes and voice-cloning are already bypassing identity checks.

WHITE

Defence is catching up — AI adoption is real — but a 90.6% talent gap is the binding constraint, and most banks still lack a CISO.

POLICY

Law and enforcement are strengthening fast — real fines, a 72-hour reporting rule, a proposed national council — but Nigeria still trails regional peers (Tier 3 GCI).
